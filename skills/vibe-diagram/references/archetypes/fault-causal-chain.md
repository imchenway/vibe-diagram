# Archetype: Fault Causal Chain

Use to explain why a fault occurred and how the proposed repair closes it.

## Recognizable shape

- Trace symptom and observed evidence toward a confirmed cause or clearly marked hypothesis.
- Continue the chain through impact, repair boundary, and verification.
- Distinguish observed, inferred, proposed, unresolved, and verified claims visually.
- Alternative hypotheses show supporting or refuting evidence instead of being presented as facts.
- Before/after subviews are useful only when both remain real diagrams.

## Avoid

- a root-cause essay inside one node;
- placing all evidence in a bottom card pile;
- declaring a cause from a health check alone;
- hiding the repair or verification path behind a click.

## Product-manager reading

Keep the symptom, user or business impact, confirmed cause or explicit hypothesis, repair, and acceptance verification on the primary chain. Raw logs and source locations support those facts without replacing them.
