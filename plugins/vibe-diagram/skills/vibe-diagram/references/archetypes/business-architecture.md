# Archetype: Business Architecture

Use for capabilities, domains, ownership, rules, value, and structural dependencies. Do not use when the story is primarily a time-ordered process.

## Recognizable shape

- Organize visible domains or ownership boundaries as regions rather than steps.
- Place capabilities, actors, business objects, and rules inside or beside the boundaries they actually belong to.
- Draw value, ownership, constraint, or dependency relations directly on the canvas.
- Let the number, depth, and orientation of regions follow the evidence.

## Avoid

- start/end events or a numbered event chain;
- a generic layered system-architecture stack;
- one card per paragraph with relationships moved to a ledger.

This is a grammar reference, not a content outline.

## Product-manager reading

Make the business domains, capabilities, ownership, value, and constraints understandable before exposing systems or modules. Technical boundaries appear only when they explain a real responsibility or dependency.
